calixto-ros-bot.md
------------------

Two wheel differential drive robot equiped with a single 2d 360 degree LiDAR, 2 emergency stop buttons in front and back side along with bumper sensor. 

Dimensions:-
Length: 780mm
Width: 480mm
Height: 180mm

Wheel diameter: 170mm
Wheel Thickness: 45mm
Wheel Separation: 350mm

2D LiDAR situated on top centre 80mm away from the front side of the AMR. Wheels are situated in the middle of the length.
